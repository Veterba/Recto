# Beta feedback

From the 12 TestFlight testers, collected 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-28.

- Weekly goals — asked by 7 of 12 testers. By far the most requested.
- A way to pause a habit while travelling — 4 testers.
- Bigger check button in the widget — 3 testers.
- Two testers found the amber accent too bright in dark mode.
- One tester wants Norwegian translation (Kari, from Bergen).
