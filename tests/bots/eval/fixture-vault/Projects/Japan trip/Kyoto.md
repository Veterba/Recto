*A note about the heart of the trip*

==Kyoto rewards the early riser: by seven the gates of Fushimi Inari are yours, by ten they belong to everyone.==

Five nights in a guesthouse near Gojo, walking distance to Higashiyama and one stop from the station.

## Where to go

### Fushimi Inari
Start at dawn, climb past the Yotsutsuji crossing for the view, and come down a different path. Two hours if you don't stop, four if you do.

### Arashiyama
The bamboo grove is five minutes long and best at 7:30. Then Okochi Sanso garden, and lunch by the river.

### Tofuku-ji
The Tsutenkyo bridge over a valley of maples. Peak colour is usually the last week of November. Expect a queue; it moves.

### Gion and Pontocho
Evenings only. Hanamikoji in the rain is the most beautiful street I know.

## Practical

- Buses are slow in leaf season. Walk or take the Keihan line.
- Temples close early, often at 16:30.
- Nishiki market for breakfast snacks, never for a sit-down meal.
