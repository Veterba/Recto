*A note about the four Tokyo days*

We stay near Shibuya: loud, but the trains go everywhere and jet lag is easier when the street is awake at 5 am.

## Plans

- **teamLab Planets**: book the 10:00 slot, wear shorts, you walk through water
- **Ghibli Museum** in Mitaka: tickets go on sale on the 10th of the month before, and they go fast
- **Yanaka**: the old-town walk, cats and temples, the quietest afternoon of the week
- **Omoide Yokocho** for yakitori under the train tracks

## Notes from last time

- The Shibuya crossing is better from above. The Starbucks window is a cliché for a reason.
- Suica on the phone, top up with a card. No queue at ticket machines.
