*A note about how the trip hangs together, written as I go*

Thirteen days, four bases, one rule: ==no more than one big thing a day==. The first trip was a sprint and I came home needing a holiday. This time I want slow mornings, long walks and at least one evening with nothing planned at all.

## The shape of it

Tokyo for four nights to land and adjust, one night in a ryokan in Hakone, then the long middle stretch in [[Kyoto]], and Osaka at the end for food and an easy flight home from Kansai. The day-by-day lives in [[Itinerary]], the money in [[Budget]].

## Why late November

Kyoto's maples turn in the last week of November. The crowds are real, but so is the colour, and early starts solve most of the crowd problem.
