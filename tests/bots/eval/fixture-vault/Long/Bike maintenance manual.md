# Bike maintenance manual

My own manual for the road bike (Canyon Endurace, bought 2024). Everything I do myself, and the numbers I keep forgetting.

## Chain

The chain is the part that wears fastest. Measure it every 500 km with a chain checker. The wear limit for an 11-speed chain is 0.5% elongation; for 8- and 9-speed chains it is 0.75%. Replace it at the limit, or the cassette and chainrings wear with it. Clean with a degreaser, dry it, then one drop of oil on each roller and wipe off the excess after ten minutes.

Write down the date and the kilometres every time something is replaced, so the next service can be planned instead of guessed. Most problems announce themselves early as a noise, a skip or a soft feel, and are cheap to fix at that stage. When in doubt, check torque values on the part itself before tightening, because carbon parts crack when over-tightened. ## Cassette

The cassette is 11–34 with eleven sprockets. A worn cassette skips under load on the smallest sprockets first. If a new chain skips on the old cassette, the cassette is worn too. The lockring is tightened to 40 Nm.

## Brakes

Hydraulic disc brakes, Shimano. Pads are resin; replace them when the pad material is under 0.5 mm. Bleed the brakes once a year with mineral oil, never DOT fluid. Rotors are 160 mm front and rear; replace a rotor under 1.5 mm thickness.

## Gears

Rear derailleur indexing: if the chain hesitates going to a bigger sprocket, turn the barrel adjuster anticlockwise a quarter turn. Replace the gear cable every year; it is cheap and fixes most shifting problems.

## Schedule

Every ride: tyre pressure. Every week: clean and oil the chain. Every month: check the chain, brake pads and bolts. Every year: cables, brake bleed, hub service.
