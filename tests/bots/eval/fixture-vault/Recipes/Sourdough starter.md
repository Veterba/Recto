# Sourdough starter

Current routine (since September 2026):
- Feed once a day at a 1:5:5 ratio: 10 g starter, 50 g flour, 50 g water.
- Flour: half rye, half white bread flour.
- Keep at room temperature, ready 6–8 hours after feeding, when it has doubled.
- Before a long trip: feed 1:10:10 and put it in the fridge.
