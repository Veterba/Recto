# Lisbon trip

12–16 June 2026, with Anna.
Stayed in Alfama. Best meal: grilled sardines at a tiny place near Graça. Pastéis de nata at Manteigaria, not Belém (shorter queue, just as good).
Tram 28 is a tourist trap in summer: walk instead. Day trip to Sintra: go early, Pena Palace at opening time.
Total spent: about 1,400 EUR for two.
