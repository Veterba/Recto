# Tomatoes

Varieties this year: Sungold (cherry), Black Krim, San Marzano.
Sow indoors in mid-March, plant out after the last frost, around 20 May.
Water at the base in the morning, never the leaves. Feed with tomato feed every 10 days once the first truss sets.
Pinch out side shoots on cordon types. Sungold split after heavy rain in August: water more evenly.
