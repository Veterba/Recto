a feature or a substantial change bumps MINOR and resets PATCH; a fix or a small change bumps PATCH.
Decided from each commit's diff (`git show --shortstat`) and message, not from its old number.
Only the prefix changes: the text after it is byte-for-byte the old text after the old prefix
(messages that had no prefix keep their whole text). ⚠ marks the rows worth a second look.
