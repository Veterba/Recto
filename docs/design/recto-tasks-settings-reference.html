Recto settings and tasks

Second round: one direction for each screen, built from your references and the parts you liked. Everything is clickable.

Tasks. Columns run the full height of the window, and adding a column is a slim dashed strip at the end of the row, so nothing looks like it falls off the edge. Cards show priority, dates, two lines of description and a footer with checklist and links. Click a card to open it in the side panel. Widgets above the board can be added or removed: a week calendar with date ranges for tasks and whole columns, progress, and what is due soon. Comments and co-authors are shown faded: they are for later.

Recto

Settings as a floating glass window. Drag it by the title bar, resize it from any edge or the corner, double-click the title bar to put it back in the middle. It stays translucent, so you see the change land in the app behind it while it is open. Sections sit in a sidebar instead of the top tab row, which already runs off the edge. Accent is blue in light theme and violet in dark, like the app.
